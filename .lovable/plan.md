

# RecallX Marketing — Website Plan

## Brand Identity
A clean, professional, corporate-modern digital marketing agency website. Confident, data-driven, and performance-focused — not flashy or overly creative.

## Design System
- **Colors**: Off-white background (#F8F9FB), Charcoal text (#111111), Performance Blue accent (#2F5DA8) following the 70-20-10 rule
- **Typography**: Bold modern sans-serif headings (Montserrat/Outfit style), clean body text (Inter style)
- **Layout**: Centered white container (max-width 1200px), rounded corners, subtle shadow, generous spacing

## Pages & Sections (Single-Page Website)

### 1. Hero Section
- Large multi-line headline: **"Scale Faster. Convert Better. Dominate Digital."** with accent blue on key words
- Subheading about ROI and measurable growth
- Two CTAs: "Request Strategy Call" (blue) and "View Our Work" (outline)
- Subtle fade-in animation

### 2. Services Section (6-Card Grid)
- Paid Ads & Performance Marketing
- SEO & Organic Growth
- Funnel & Conversion Optimization
- Website & App Development
- AI Content & Script Strategy
- Reputation & Review Management
- Each card with a line icon (blue accent), title, and short description

### 3. Why Choose RecallX
- Grid layout with four differentiators: Data-Driven Execution, ROI-Focused Strategy, Fast Response Team, Transparent Reporting
- Animated count-up numbers: 120% Average Growth, 3X ROAS Scaling, 24-Hour Strategy Response

### 4. Process Section (Horizontal Timeline)
- Four steps: Audit → Strategy → Execute → Scale
- Each with icon and short explanation
- Scroll-triggered reveal animation

### 5. Case Studies
- Before/After metric comparisons (e.g., 1.2X → 3.8X ROAS, 240% Lead Growth, 42% Cost Reduction)
- Key numbers highlighted in blue

### 6. Inquiry / Lead Form
- Professional contact form: Name, Business Type, Monthly Budget, Goal, Message
- CTA: "Get Custom Growth Plan"
- Clean, premium feel

### 7. Footer
- Charcoal background (#111111) with white text
- Quick Links, Services, Contact Email, WhatsApp button, Copyright

## Animations
- Fade-in on scroll with slight upward motion
- Smooth number counter animations
- Button hover glow effects
- No flashy or heavy effects

## Technical Notes
- Single-page React app, no backend needed for initial version
- The inquiry form will show a success toast (no email delivery without a backend — can be added later)
- Fully responsive for mobile and desktop
